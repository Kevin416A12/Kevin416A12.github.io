## 0.7.1 — 2026-11-15

- Story intro is now one animated panel at a time (progressive disclosure) with Next / Start mission.
- Ana sits on the scene (top-left), so the decision sheet uses the full width.
- Progress bar: "Step 2 of 7 · 5 steps left", with ORION and Base Alpha as milestones.
- Costs are explicit: every value carries its sign, and selecting a card shows before → after on each bar.
- Go is outlined until a choice is made. Secondary buttons step back.
- Stronger default glass blur; "Low effects" still turns it off. Smaller "AI-generated" label.
- Ana's browser voice made more robust; "Test Ana's voice" in Settings.

## 0.7.0 — 2026-11-15 (iteration v7.3)

- New name and identity: Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer — Saving Rover Orion. Team logo with an "AI-generated" label.
- New story: SCOUT (rescue rover) saves ORION (science rover). Three story panels on the start screen. The crew stays safe in Base Alpha.
- Resources are now Time, Battery (%) and Health (%). Oxygen is removed.
- Seven redesigned decisions: signal booster, solar storm shelters (crater or boulder), check-up, antenna fix with an honest range, locked booster rescue, uphill journey home.
- Option cards with generated gain/cost tags, same-scale comparison bars, exact values and a signal row; one "Go" pill.
- NASA data tags in the scene and on cards, with a one-sentence popover and a link into the data drawer.
- ORION's-signal countdown, solar storm pop-up with frame glow, repair pop-up, win/lose banner colours.
- Music (four states, crossfade, ducking), eight sound effects, three audio manifests.
- New palette from the logo, Michroma titles, Phosphor icons. Bottom button row removed; Ask Ana lives on her bubble.
